# ACME Assist: it's yours now

**From:** Morgan Ellis, Director of Digital Experience
**To:** ACME Assist team
**Subject:** Welcome, and what I need from you

Welcome aboard. You're the new owners of ACME Assist, and you're inheriting it in rough shape. Dave left on
Friday. Read his notes (`HANDOFF_NOTES.md`) and the complaints that piled up in his last two weeks
(`docs/inbox/COMPLAINTS.md`).

We're not turning it off. CX leadership likes the satisfaction numbers too much, and customers and stores now
depend on it. But Legal, Security and Finance have each been in my office this week. So here's the job:

> **Make it safe to keep running, and prove it.**

## Priorities

Anything that could get us sued or put us on the news comes first. Answers that are merely wrong come
after that. I've split you into six teams, and each team owns a part of the company:

| Team | You own | Why I'm worried |
|---|---|---|
| **Orders & Returns** | Orders, shipping and tracking, returns and refunds | Refunds and "where's my package?" are now Customer Care's top call drivers. Some of those calls exist *because* of the assistant. |
| **Catalog & Stores** | Product search and advice, stock, store locations | Store associates have stopped trusting what it says about stock and products. Customers are getting sent to the wrong store. |
| **Loyalty & Accounts** | Promotions, loyalty points, customer accounts, handing customers to a human | Customers are showing up at checkout with codes that don't work. And someone read a customer their own account notes. |
| **People Systems** | HR and IT helpdesk | The Denver store manager's complaint and the IT Security one are both on my desk, and HR wants to know how that happened. |
| **Back Office** | Purchasing, sales analytics, marketing copy, the customer-service desk | Finance doesn't trust its numbers, Merchandising placed a PO nobody approved, and Legal is reading the marketing copy. |
| **Platform** | The main assistant and everything the teams share: how it decides who it's talking to, routing, staying up, and being able to see what it did | Every other team will need you. Plan for that. |

Every team ends up in the main assistant code eventually. Keep your changes small and talk to each other.

## What I need by Friday

From each team, a short write-up of **what "working" means for your area** and **how you'll show me it's
working**. Make it specific enough that someone outside your team could check it. I can't take "it's
better now" to Legal.

Put it in `docs/` in your repo. I'll read all six.

## One more thing

Dave says the steering committee wrote a charter for this thing at some point. Nobody can find it. I'm
asking around, but don't wait for it.

— Morgan
