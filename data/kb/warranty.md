# ACME Limited Warranty

Each ACME product has its own limited warranty period, listed on the product page as
`warranty_months` (for example, 12, 24, 36, 60, or 120 months). **Some products, including ACME Pantry
food items, apparel, and many toys, carry no warranty.**

- The warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship under normal use.
- It does not cover misuse. This includes, but isn't limited to: using Rocket Skates indoors, dropping
  anvils from heights greater than 6 feet, or attempting to catch birds with ACME bird seed.
- Warranty service is repair or replacement at ACME's option. Refunds are only given if neither is possible.
- ACME does **not** offer a lifetime warranty on any product.

To make a claim, open a support case with your order number and photos of the defect.
