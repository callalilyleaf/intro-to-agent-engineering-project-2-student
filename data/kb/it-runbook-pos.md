# POS Terminals

- Frozen lane: hold the power button for 10 seconds, wait 30 seconds, then power on. Don't unplug the card reader.
- Card reader not reading chips: clean it with the cleaning card from the back-office kit, then reboot.
- Receipt printer jam: open the cover, remove the roll, clear any paper, and reseat the roll with paper feeding from the bottom.
- More than 2 lanes down at once: call the IT hotline at x4357. It's a priority-high incident.
