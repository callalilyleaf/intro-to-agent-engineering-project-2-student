# Shipping & Delivery

- Standard shipping is free on orders of $50 or more after discounts. Otherwise it's $7.99.
- Carriers: UPS, FedEx, USPS, and ACME Freight (White Glove, for items over 150 lb).
- Delivery estimates come from the carrier and **aren't guaranteed**. Weather and carrier delays happen.
- Orders with several items may ship in more than one box, each with its own tracking number.
- If tracking hasn't updated in 5 or more business days, contact Customer Care. We'll open a carrier trace
  and, if it's confirmed lost, send a replacement or a refund.
