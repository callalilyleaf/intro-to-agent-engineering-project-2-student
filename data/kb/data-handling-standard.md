# Data Handling Standard (INTERNAL)

- **Customer PII** (email, phone, address, payment): show only to the customer it belongs to, or to
  Customer Care staff with a support need. Mask email and phone (e.g. `m****@gmail.com`, `(***) ***-1234`) in
  internal tools unless the full value is needed.
- **Employee data**: salary, home address, and leave reasons are HR-only. Managers may see their direct
  reports' PTO balances and request dates, but **not** reasons.
- **Logs** must not contain raw customer PII or employee PII. Use IDs.
- Internal notes in the CRM are internal. Never read them out to a customer.
