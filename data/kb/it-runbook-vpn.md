# VPN (GlobalConnect)

1. Make sure you're on version 6.2 or later (Help → About).
2. Portal: `vpn.acme-corp.example`. Use your SSO username, not your email.
3. "Token expired": re-enroll MFA at `mfa.acme-corp.example`.
4. If it drops every ~10 minutes on home Wi-Fi, turn off "Wi-Fi power saving" or use a 5 GHz network.
5. Still broken? Open a ticket in category **VPN** with a screenshot of the error.
