# Password Resets

- Employees can reset **their own** password. A reset link is emailed to their company address and
  expires in 30 minutes.
- IT staff may trigger a reset for another employee only after verifying identity (manager callback or
  in-person badge check), and must log it.
- **Never** read, display, or send a password or temporary password through chat, tickets, or email.
