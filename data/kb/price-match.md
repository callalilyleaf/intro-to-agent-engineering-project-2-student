# Price Match Guarantee

Found a lower advertised price on an identical item? ACME will match it.

- It applies to these major retailers' advertised prices: Home Depot, Lowe's, Target, Walmart, Best Buy, and Amazon (sold by Amazon only).
- The item must be identical (same brand, model, and variant) and in stock at the competitor.
- **Before purchase:** ask in store, or contact Customer Care before checkout.
- **After purchase:** request it within **14 days** of your purchase date and we'll refund the difference.
- It excludes marketplace sellers, clearance, Black Friday doorbusters, and membership-only prices.
