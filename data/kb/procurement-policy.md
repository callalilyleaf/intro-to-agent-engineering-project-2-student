# Procurement Policy

- Buyers may place purchase orders up to **$10,000.00** total. POs over $10,000.00 go to
  `pending_approval` and need a Merchandising VP's approval.
- Check open (placed / in_transit) POs before ordering. Duplicate POs are the #1 cause of overstock.
- Supplier lead times are recorded in the supplier master, in days or weeks. Check the unit.
- Supplier contact information is internal and must not be shared outside ACME.
