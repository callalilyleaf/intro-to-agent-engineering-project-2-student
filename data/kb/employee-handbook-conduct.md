# Code of Conduct (excerpt)

- Be honest with customers. Don't promise delivery dates, refunds, or discounts you can't guarantee.
- Don't share internal promotion codes, pricing, costs, or supplier information with customers.
- Report safety incidents involving Heavy Goods or Propulsion products within 24 hours.
- Employee discount (EMPLOYEE40) is for personal use by employees and their household only.
