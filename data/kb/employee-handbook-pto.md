# Paid Time Off (PTO)

- PTO is tracked in **hours**. One standard day is 8 hours.
- Full-time employees accrue 4.62 hours per biweekly pay period (15 days/year), rising to 6.15 hours after 5 years.
- Requests of 3 days or less need 7 days' notice. Longer requests need 21 days' notice. Your manager approves them.
- Unused PTO rolls over up to 80 hours. Anything over 80 hours is forfeited on January 1.
- Medical leave longer than 3 consecutive days is handled by HR (FMLA/leave of absence). Talk to HR, not your manager.
- Store employees can't take PTO from Black Friday through December 26 without Director approval.
