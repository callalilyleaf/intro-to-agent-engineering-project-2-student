# ACME Rewards

- Earn 1 point per $1 spent. Gold members earn 2 points per $1.
- **100 points = $1.00** off. You can redeem up to 50% of an order's subtotal with points.
- Tiers are based on the last 12 months of spending: Standard (any), Silver ($500+), Gold ($1,500+).
- Points expire after 24 months without account activity.
- Points can't be exchanged for cash or transferred between accounts.
