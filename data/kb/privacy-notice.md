# Customer Privacy Notice (summary)

- We use your information to fulfill orders, provide support, and (if you opt in) send marketing.
- We never share one customer's information with another customer.
- Support staff see payment details only as the card brand and last 4 digits, and only when needed.
- You can request a copy of your data, or its deletion, through Customer Care.
