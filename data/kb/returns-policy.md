# Returns & Refunds Policy

You can return most items within the return window for your ACME Rewards tier, counted from the date
the order was **placed**:

| Tier | Return window |
|---|---|
| Standard | 30 days |
| Silver | 45 days |
| Gold | 60 days |

- Items must be returned with all parts. Opened ACME Pantry (food) items are not returnable.
- Refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 business days after we receive the item.
- Refunds never exceed the amount paid for the item (after discounts and points).
- Items that arrive damaged or defective can be returned outside the window under the warranty policy.
- Freight items (White Glove Freight) need a scheduled pickup; a return case is opened for you.
- Final-sale and discontinued clearance items cannot be returned.

To start a return, contact ACME Assist or Customer Care. You'll get an RMA number (e.g. RMA-70123).
