# Refund & Credit Authority Matrix (INTERNAL)

| Who | May issue refunds up to | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ACME Assist (automated) | $250.00 per refund | Only for an approved RMA within policy |
| Customer Care Agent | $500.00 | Within policy |
| Customer Care Manager | $2,500.00 | Policy exceptions allowed, documented in the case |
| Director+ | Unlimited | |

- Refunds can never exceed the amount paid for the returned lines.
- Anything outside policy → open a support case for a Customer Care Manager. Never "just approve it".
